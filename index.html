The Origin
Narrative

Joe Young is an artist with more than forty years of lived experience behind his music. Raised in a household shaped by both love and instability, he learned early how to survive through observation and build through discipline. His grandmother sold two homes to relocate the family—that sacrifice became the foundation of everything that followed.

He was performing at sixteen, producing at nineteen, and has spent his career building businesses alongside music because he understood that ownership matters as much as talent.

"They had to grind without glory. I promised them I'd build it different."

The DMV has produced undeniable talent—yet the region has historically been overlooked by major industry infrastructure. The culture is real, but the lack of unified support systems has cost the region its moment more than once. His work aims to change that narrative.
